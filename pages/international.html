Terms & Conditions

  • All packages are billed in USD; EUR amounts shown are approximate and reconfirmed at the day's exchange rate before invoicing.
  • International travel, visa, flights, and accommodation for the crew are billed separately and quoted per destination.
  • All packages are customizable according to your preferences and event schedule.
  • For booking confirmation, 50% advance payment is required. The remaining balance is due before the crew's departure for the event.
  • Final delivery: within four to six weeks for international events.
  • Within one week of the event, the client must send song selections for video editing.
  • If you prefer not to have your photos & videos posted on social media, an additional 20% of the package cost will apply.
  • Extra charges apply if the agreed time limit is exceeded on the day.
